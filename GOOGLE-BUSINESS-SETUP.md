# Google Business Profile: setup guide

A Google Business Profile puts Cheesy Bites on **Google Maps** and in **Google Search** results, with photos, opening hours, directions and a link to the website. It's free.

Only the business owner can create it, because Google verifies ownership. It takes about 15 minutes, plus waiting for verification.

---

## Step 1: Create the profile

1. Go to **https://business.google.com** and sign in with the Google account that should own the profile. A business account such as cheesy.bites11@gmail.com is best.
2. Click **Add business → Create a business**.
3. Enter the details below. Copy and paste them so the spelling matches the website exactly.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Business name | `Cheesy Bites` |
| Business category | `Fast food restaurant` |
| Additional categories | `Snack bar`, `Frozen food manufacturer` |
| Do you want to add a location customers can visit? | **Yes** |
| Country | Myanmar |
| Street address | `No.45, Pyay Road, B-19/20/22, Taw Win Center` |
| Township / City | `Dagon Township, Yangon` |
| Region | `Yangon Region` |
| Postal code | `11191` |
| Do you also serve customers outside this location? | **Yes**, add Yangon (delivery via foodpanda) |
| Phone number | *(your shop phone number)* |
| Website | `https://www.cheesybites.com.mm/` |

4. Drag the map pin exactly onto the Taw Win Center shop.

## Step 2: Verify

Google will ask you to verify ownership by phone, SMS, email, video, or a postcard to the address. Follow its instructions. The profile goes public after verification.

## Step 4: After it's live

1. On the profile, click **Share** and copy the link (it looks like `https://g.page/...` or `https://maps.app.goo.gl/...`).
2. **Send the link to your web developer.** It will be added to the website's Contact section as a "See us on Google" / review button, replacing the plain map search.
3. Ask happy customers to leave a **Google review**, and reply to every review, good or bad.
4. Repeat Steps 1–3 for other company outlets (PinLon Hills, City Mall) if you want each to appear on Maps.
