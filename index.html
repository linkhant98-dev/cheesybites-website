About Us

Who We Are

A Myanmar snack brand bringing quality cheesy bites to retail shelves, food courts, wholesale channels and franchise locations.

Cheesy Bites started in early 2020 out of a love for snacks. Since then it has grown into a community of food lovers, partners and entrepreneurs, all sharing one idea: Food For Everyone. Quality snacks for everyday customers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Cheesy Bites franchise cost?

There are three packages to match what you already have: Package 2 (from 13xxxxx Ks), Package 1 (18xxxxx Ks) and the all-in-one Full Package (32xxxxx Ks). Send an enquiry or message us for the exact price.

What's included in the package?

Every package includes the 3-year brand licence, a full branding kit (stickers, vinyl, menu stand, corndog standee, 2 staff uniforms), opening food stock, launch advertising, and training with ongoing support. The Full Package also includes the branded food cart and kitchen equipment.

Do I need cooking experience or a chef?

No. Our products come frozen and ready to fry, and take about 3 minutes to cook. We train you to cook, serve and sell, so you can run the shop yourself without hiring a chef.

Where do I get my supplies?

Cheesy Bites supplies ready-to-fry frozen products to your shop regularly. You buy only what you sell, so there's less waste and you're protected from daily changes in raw material prices.

Can I open in a shop instead of a food cart?

Yes. Many partners open in a shop room, kiosk or mall counter. If you don't use the food cart, you'll need to build one cash counter (4 ft × 2 ft) in the Cheesy Bites colours and design.

How long does the franchise last?

The franchise fee covers the right to use the Cheesy Bites name and brand for 3 years. Contact us for renewal details.

Can I open in my town?

Cheesy Bites already has outlets in 21+ cities across Myanmar, from Yangon and Mandalay to Lashio and Mawlamyine. Tell us your town and location in the enquiry form, and we'll let you know if it's available.

Do you help with marketing?

Yes. Every package includes launch advertising designed for your shop and promoted to customers near you, plus free use of our design files and product photos for your own posts.
