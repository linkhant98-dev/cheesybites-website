About Us

Who We Are

A Myanmar snack brand bringing quality cheesy bites to retail shelves, food courts, wholesale channels and franchise locations.

Cheesy Bites started in early 2020 out of a love for snacks. Since then it has grown into a community of food lovers, partners and entrepreneurs, all sharing one idea: Food For Everyone. Quality snacks for everyday customers.

  • ✅ Registered company & tax registered
  • 🧪 Food-safety lab tested

Why franchisees choose Cheesy Bites

👩‍🍳

No chef or extra staff needed

Staff are hard to find these days. Our frozen products are ready to fry, so you can cook and sell them yourself in about 3 minutes.

💸

Less waste, steadier costs

Raw material prices change every day. Making snacks from scratch means waste and risk. We supply ready products, so you buy only what you sell.

⭐

A brand people already know
